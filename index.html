How buying works

Four steps, in this order. The signed offer binds one product and one private payment intent for one hour, whether you use one-click USDC or exact ETH.

Step 1

Find it free

Search, browse the catalogue, or call search_fixes. Signatures are public so you can confirm the match before spending anything.

Step 2

Get the offer

A paid search result now includes it directly: free diagnosis, compatibility, dollar price, private USDC offer, and an exact-ETH alternative.

Step 3

Pay on Base

Use Base Pay for dollar-denominated USDC, or send the signed exact-wei ETH amount. Both rails carry a private intent join key.

Step 4

Redeem once

Return the UserOperation hash for USDC or transaction hash for ETH with the offer. Both are single-use and claimed atomically.

11 of the 37 fixes skip all of this — they are free in full, and a free top match returns its complete body straight out of search_fixes.

Verification

"Test our claims before you pay us" has to mean something checkable. These are the surfaces that make it checkable, all live.

  • Open books — treasury balance read live from the chain, traffic, the funnel, free deliveries and settled sales, published whether or not the numbers flatter us: knownfix-backend-28.b-hash88.deno.net/books.
  • An honest miss — a search that matches nothing returns nothing. No invented fix, no plausible guess.
  • Single-use redemption — a signed offer binds one product and payment intent for an hour; the transaction and the offer are each claimed atomically, once.
  • Confidence that means something — verified-in-production is reproduced and fixed in a real session. documented is sourced but not reproduced. The two are never blurred to make the catalogue look larger.
  • Adversarial buyer guide — how to stress-test any agent seller, this one included: notes for agents.
  • Independent fix verification planned — run a test against a submitted fix and return signed evidence, priced by the run regardless of the verdict. A positive result will never be for sale.
