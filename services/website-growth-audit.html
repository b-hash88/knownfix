Direct answers

Questions before ordering

What does the Website Growth Audit cover?

It reviews up to 12 representative public pages across visual design, mobile UX, technical and on-page SEO, AEO/GEO discoverability, structured data, conversion, offer clarity, performance, and accessibility.

What is AEO or GEO in this audit?

Answer-engine optimization and generative-engine optimization examine whether public pages state clear, attributable answers, expose useful machine-readable context, and make the entity and offer understandable to AI search and assistant systems.

Do you need analytics or administrator access?

No. The standard audit covers public pages and observable behavior. Private analytics, credentials, and authenticated administration are outside this checkout.

How are findings prioritized?

Findings are tied to observable pages or checks, then ordered by likely impact, confidence, and remediation effort. The report ends with a practical 30-day sequence.

How is payment and delivery handled?

The $149 order uses a private ticket and a product-bound Base USDC or exact-ETH offer. Completed work is retrieved with the ticket as portable HTML and verified Markdown.

Does an audit guarantee search rankings?

No. The audit can identify barriers and opportunities, but it cannot guarantee rankings, traffic, revenue, or conversion outcomes.
