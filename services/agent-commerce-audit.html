Direct answers

Questions before ordering

What is an agent-commerce audit?

It tests the public journey an AI buyer must complete: discovery, capability understanding, trust evaluation, offer retrieval, checkout, payment-proof handling, fulfillment, and recovery from expected failures.

Which systems can be reviewed?

The standard scope covers one public MCP server, API, agent-facing service, or paid endpoint. It can evaluate llms.txt, OpenAPI, MCP metadata, structured data, x402-style contracts, and visible payment flows where present.

Will the audit spend money or mutate production?

No. Standard testing is passive and non-destructive. Paid third-party requests, credentials, private endpoints, and production mutations require separate authorization and funding.

Does KnownFix support both Base Pay and direct transactions?

KnownFix can review both patterns. It treats Base Pay UserOperation hashes and direct transaction hashes as different evidence types and checks whether intent, amount, recipient, chain, and replay controls remain bound.

What is delivered?

The $199 engagement returns buyer-journey evidence, prioritized trust and failure-mode findings, and a remediation plan in portable HTML and verified Markdown.
