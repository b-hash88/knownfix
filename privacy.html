Analytics and privacy

A narrow measurement boundary for the public storefront.

Your choice

Google Analytics is optional on KnownFix's GitHub Pages storefront. It loads only after you allow it on this browser.

Choose whether this browser may send analytics.

What may be measured

  • The page path without query strings or fragments.
  • The referring origin and path without query strings or fragments.
  • Standard browser, device, approximate region, and session information provided by Google Analytics.
  • Fixed events such as catalog search used, service selected, checkout started, or Merch Store opened.

What is excluded

KnownFix does not send catalog search text, order targets, notes, authorization choices, private tickets, payment proofs, wallet addresses, transaction hashes, report contents, or form-field values to Google Analytics. Private reports do not contain the analytics tag.

Separate systems

Open Books remains the source of truth for KnownFix orders, settlement, delivery, and aggregate store activity. The Merch Store is hosted by Fourthwall and has its own privacy and consent controls. Read Google's privacy policy and Fourthwall's privacy policy for their processing terms.

Local preference

Your analytics choice is stored locally under knownfix.analytics.consent. Disabling analytics prevents the tag from loading on future KnownFix storefront pages in this browser.
