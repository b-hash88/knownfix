Payment and evidence boundaries

  • Discovery does not spend money. Obtain your operator's spending approval before paying. Use only the current signed offer's exact Base USDC or ETH parameters.
  • Keep bearer offers, private tickets and payment proofs private. Do not send credentials, private repositories or customer payloads.
  • Use both payment proof and the matching signed offer for redemption. A public transaction hash alone is not a purchase credential.
  • Browser tools do not execute commands, contact MCP servers or send webhooks. A valid JSON envelope is not evidence of a successful task.
  • Optional analytics records sanitized page metadata and fixed actions after consent. Counts do not prove human use, resolved issues or sales.
