When to investigate further

A passing envelope is not a completed task
Keep the transport status, JSON-RPC result and tool-level outcome separate. The tool-error example is intentionally valid at the envelope layer.
Streaming frames do not parse
The inspector expects one JSON object, not HTTP headers, SSE framing, a batch or a whole transcript.
